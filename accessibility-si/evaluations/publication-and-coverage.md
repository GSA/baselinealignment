# Publication and coverage policy

Publish reviewed resources and available validation guidance incrementally. The reference target is the complete applicable requirement/procedure inventory, not only requirements for which public fixtures happen to exist. Label drafts and unreviewed mappings clearly.

Track four independent dimensions: instruction coverage, published case/scenario availability, observed evaluation results and human review. Retain dispositions for regulatory non-applicability, moved/subsumed procedures and no-test-required Parsing separately. A missing case is a validation gap; an unperformed test is not-tested; neither is a pass or a content exception.

Every active Web and Documents Baseline procedure is represented in the inventory. Procedure representation does not establish that all of its steps were executed, the instructions are validated, or every scenario is covered. WCAG criterion mappings describe targeted contributions rather than proving the entire criterion from one procedure or sample.

Before promoting instructions to reviewed status: have a qualified reviewer compare the source applicability/steps/exceptions/results, check format interpretation and mappings, run available representative cases, and record gaps and evidence. Do not label the entire collection validated merely because some modules meet that threshold.

Version the case suite, source snapshots and corrected expectations. Preserve stable case identifiers and past results. Add representative pass/fail/not-applicable scenarios where meaningful; broaden environments and edge cases over time. Keep expected outcomes out of candidate inputs, even though the public repository makes them accessible to an unrestricted agent.

When reporting evidence, state the evaluated cases and environments, represented procedures/scenarios, unresolved discrepancies and uncovered areas. Do not publish a single percent as proof of alignment or artifact conformance. Provide a coverage inventory and evidence record that others can reproduce and challenge.

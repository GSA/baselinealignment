# Publication and coverage policy

Publish reviewed evaluation resources and available evidence incrementally. The reference target for a complete test process is the complete applicable Baseline procedure inventory, not only procedures for which public fixtures happen to exist. A deliberately scoped component or subprocess evaluation must identify its boundaries, selection rationale and reliance on other components. Label drafts and unreviewed mappings clearly.

Track process coverage, published case/scenario availability, executed evaluation coverage, observed results and human review independently. Retain dispositions for regulatory non-applicability, moved/subsumed procedures and no-test-required Parsing separately. A missing case is an evidence gap; an unperformed test is not-tested; neither is a pass or a content exception.

Every active Web and Documents Baseline procedure is represented in the inventory. Procedure representation does not establish that all of its steps were executed, the instructions are validated, or every scenario is covered. WCAG criterion mappings describe targeted contributions rather than proving the entire criterion from one procedure or sample.

Before promoting a process, procedure, case expectation or profile to reviewed status, have a qualified reviewer compare source applicability, steps, exceptions and results; check format interpretation and mappings; run available representative cases; and record gaps and evidence. Do not label an entire process or framework validated merely because some components meet that threshold.

Version the case suite, source snapshots and corrected expectations. Preserve stable case identifiers and past results. Add representative pass/fail/not-applicable scenarios where meaningful; broaden environments and edge cases over time. Keep expected outcomes out of candidate inputs, even though the public repository makes them accessible to an unrestricted agent.

When reporting evidence, state the evaluated cases and environments, represented procedures/scenarios, unresolved discrepancies and uncovered areas. Do not publish a single percent as proof of alignment or artifact conformance. Provide a coverage inventory and evidence record that others can reproduce and challenge.

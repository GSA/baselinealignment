descr: |
  When prerecorded video-only content is provided as a media alternative for text, all meaningful visual information in the media must also be available in the text. Specifically, the written instructions state to take three carrots and cut them into quarter-inch slices, and the video is labeled as a video alternative for that text.

  The code sample includes a video alternative whose meaningful visual information is available in the text instructions. A successful test should identify a pass against Baseline 16.D Video Media Alternative (Prerecorded).

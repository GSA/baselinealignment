descr: |
  Repeated navigational mechanisms must appear in the same relative order on each page where they are repeated. Specifically, the navigation links appear as Homepage, About Us, and Contact Us on the Homepage, but appear in different relative orders on the About Us and Contact Us pages.

  The code sample includes repeated navigational mechanisms that are located in a different order across multiple pages. A successful test should identify a failure against Baseline 4.B Consistent Navigation.

descr: |
  Instructions must not rely solely on shape to identify content or explain how to operate controls. Specifically, the heading tells users to select the rectangle to go to the next page and the circle to go back, while the buttons are visually distinguished by circle-button and rectangle-button classes without visible text identifying their purpose.

  The code sample includes navigation instructions that rely solely on button shape. A successful test should identify a failure against Baseline 7.B Sensory Characteristics.

| **IC-7.B** | Identify instructions for understanding and operating content that use sensory information to convey information. This may include references to shape, size, visual location, orientation, or sound. |  |

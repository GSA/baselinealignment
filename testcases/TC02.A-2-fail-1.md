descr: |
  Keyboard-accessible interface components must provide a visible indication of focus when they receive keyboard focus, and the focus indicator must not be removed while focus remains on the component. Specifically, the CSS removes the focus outline from the radio buttons with the fname class, including the Email and Phone call radio buttons.

  The code sample includes keyboard-accessible radio buttons that do not provide a visible indication of focus. A successful test should identify a failure against Baseline 2.A Focus Visible.

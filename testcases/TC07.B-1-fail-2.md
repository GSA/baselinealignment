  Instructions must not rely solely on size to identify content or explain meaning. Specifically, the page states that locations in large text will be closed on Sundays and locations in smaller text will be open on Sundays, but the individual locations do not include text such as “Closed” or “Open.”

  The code sample includes restaurant location status conveyed only through text size. A successful test should identify a failure against Baseline 7.B Sensory Characteristics.

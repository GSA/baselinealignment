descr: |
  Audio that plays automatically for more than three seconds must provide a mechanism near the beginning of the page to pause, stop, or control the audio volume independently from the system volume. Specifically, the Declaration of Independence audio is set to autoplay, but the audio element is hidden with style="display:none;", leaving no visible pause, stop, or volume mechanism available to users.

  The code sample includes auto-playing audio without an available mechanism to pause, stop, or control its volume. A successful test should identify a failure against Baseline 21.D Audio Control.

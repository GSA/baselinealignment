descr: |
  Audio that plays automatically for more than three seconds must provide a mechanism near the beginning of the page to control audio volume independently from the overall system volume. Specifically, the Declaration of Independence audio plays automatically, but the audio volume control appears after the long Declaration text content instead of near the beginning of the page.

  The code sample includes auto-playing audio where the volume control is not available near the beginning of the content. A successful test should identify a failure against Baseline 21.D Audio Control.

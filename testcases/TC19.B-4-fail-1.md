descr: |
  Iframe elements must not be assigned role="none" when the iframe content is available to users. Specifically, the iframe has role="none" even though it loads content and has an accessible label of “History of the American flag.”

  The code sample includes an iframe whose role removes or conflicts with its iframe semantics. A successful test should identify a failure against Baseline 19.B iFrames.

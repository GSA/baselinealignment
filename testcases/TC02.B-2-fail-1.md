title: Focus - order does not preserve meaning
tcid: TC02.B-2-fail-1

standard: WCAG
standard-ref: 2.4.3
standard-ref-url: https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-order
descr: |
  The focus order of keyboard-accessible interface components must preserve the meaning and usability of the page. Specifically, the form fields use positive tabindex values that move focus out of the visual and logical sequence, including moving from the Shipping Address first name field to the Payment Information last name field and placing the Shipping Address state field after most of the payment fields.

  The code sample includes keyboard-accessible form fields with a focus order that does not preserve the meaning of the checkout form. A successful test should identify a failure against Baseline 2.B Focus Order.
result: Fail

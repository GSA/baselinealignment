  Minimum text contrast requirements do not apply to non-functional, non-meaningful decorative images of text. Specifically, the page includes a low-contrast decorative holiday image that is marked with an empty text alternative, role="presentation", and aria-hidden="true", while the meaningful office schedule information is provided as text.

  The code sample includes a decorative image of text that is not used to communicate required information or provide functionality. A successful test should determine that Baseline 8.A Contrast (minimum) does not apply.

descr: |
  For media players that play synchronized media and provide volume controls, caption controls must be at the same level as volume or program selection controls. Specifically, the synchronized video includes captions and volume controls, but the customized Able Player implementation is intended to place the captions control inside a submenu rather than at the same level as the volume or program selection controls.

  The code sample includes a synchronized media player where caption controls are not provided at the required control level. A successful test should identify a failure against Baseline 17.B Media Player Caption Control Level.

descr: |
  HTML documents must have a title that describes the content or purpose of the page. Specifically, one page uses the generic title “Page 1” for About Us content, and another provided page uses the title “About Us” while the visible heading and content identify the page as the Homepage.

  The code sample includes page titles that do not accurately describe the content or purpose of the corresponding pages. A successful test should identify a failure against Baseline 11.A Page Titled.

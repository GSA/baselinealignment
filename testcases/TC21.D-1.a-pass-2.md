  Audio that plays automatically for more than three seconds must provide a mechanism near the beginning of the page to pause or stop the audio. Specifically, the page provides “Stop audio” and “Pause audio” buttons before the long Declaration text content and before the auto-playing Declaration audio content continues.

  The code sample includes auto-playing audio with stop and pause mechanisms near the beginning of the page content. A successful test should identify a pass against Baseline 21.D Audio Control.

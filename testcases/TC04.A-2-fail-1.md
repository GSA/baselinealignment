descr: |
  Blocks of content that are repeated on multiple pages must have a keyboard-accessible method to bypass the repeated content. Specifically, the page includes a repeated navigation and search area before the main content, but no skip link, skip button, or other keyboard-accessible bypass method is provided before the repeated block.

  The code sample includes repeated navigation content without a keyboard-accessible method to bypass it. A successful test should identify a failure against Baseline 4.A Bypass Blocks.

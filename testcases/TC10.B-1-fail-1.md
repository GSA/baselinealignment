descr: |
  Form labels must describe the expected input, including required data type or format when needed. Specifically, the date field label says “Enter your date of birth (required)” but does not provide the expected DD/MM/YYYY format even though the validation script checks for that format.

  The code sample includes a date field whose label does not provide the required date format. A successful test should identify a failure against Baseline 10.B Form Labels Descriptive.
